# Course2Career — AmarktAI Sales Assistant

AmarktAI is designed to remove sales administration so you can spend more time speaking to customers. Course2Career is configured for UK time (Europe/London), UK formatting and GBP.

## Your daily flow

1. **Today** — start here. New leads needing first contact are surfaced first, followed by genuinely overdue work, customer replies and tasks due today. Future work stays scheduled instead of crowding the day.
2. **Customers** — search the full customer list and open one person to see contact details, course/programme interest, Course2Career enquiry context, current and completed tasks, recent history and opportunity context where it exists.
3. **Calls** — open the selected person in the call workspace. The same customer context follows you into the call so you do not need to search the CRM again.
4. **AmarktAI** — use it for the work around the call: prepare the conversation, summarise history, identify the next step, draft a follow-up or capture a reminder.
5. **Review** — check customer-facing or consequential work before anything is allowed to execute. Draft-only work can be reviewed even when sending is offline.

## New leads

AmarktAI checks for newly arrived Genie leads in the background while you work. A new lead can alert you from any main sales screen, shows the course/programme interest when it can be identified, and gives you a direct route to open the person or prepare the first call. The same lead is not repeatedly announced every minute.

## What is safe during handover

Genie is currently connected read-only. AmarktAI can read the authorised Course2Career sales context but must not change Genie records or send customer communications during handover. Email, SMS, WhatsApp, CRM notes, task changes, opportunity/stage changes and other external writes remain offline until they are deliberately commissioned with Amelia present.

## If something looks wrong

Use **Refresh** on the affected workspace first. A local page error does not automatically mean Genie is disconnected. Only use **CRM setup** when the application specifically reports a current CRM authentication or permission problem.
